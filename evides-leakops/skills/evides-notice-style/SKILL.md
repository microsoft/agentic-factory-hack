# Resident notice checklist (SIMULATED skill for the Evides LeakOps demo)

Apply these steps in order every time you write a resident notice.

1. **Start from the facts you were given.** Place, streets, roughly how many connections. Nothing else.
2. **Dutch first.** B1 level, short sentences, address people with "u". Then English: a natural translation, never a copy.
3. **Say what residents notice:** no water or low pressure, and where.
4. **Say what is known, honestly:** a disruption of the water main. For the cause write "vermoedelijk" / "suspected" a burst. Never state the cause as certain.
5. **Say what Evides does:** "Evides werkt aan een oplossing." and that the page is kept up to date.
6. **Leave out, always:** ids and codes, pipe details, valves, technicians, costs, severity, customer names, blame.
7. **No time promises.** No repair time, duration or arrival until the schedule exists; the system adds the time later.
8. **Nothing about water safety.** Standard advice and the help line are added by the system.
9. **Check the limits:** title at most 90 characters, message at most 500.
10. **Read it once as a resident.** Would a neighbour who knows nothing about pipes understand it?
