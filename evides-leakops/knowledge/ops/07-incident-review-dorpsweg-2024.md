# INC-2024-0217: Burst on Dorpsweg, Spijkenisse (post-incident review)
Datum: 17 februari 2024 (gesimuleerd) | Segment: SEG-0412 | Pijp: DN300 cast iron, installed 1962

## What happened
At 03:40, during a frost period (-5 degrees), pressure on the Dorpsweg main fell by about 2 bar and the district inlet flow rose to more than twice the night-time normal. A crew was dispatched at 04:05.

## Timeline
- 03:40 alarm; 03:48 confirmed as a burst; 04:05 crew dispatched.
- 04:35 crew on site. **Valve V-0412-B would not close fully: it had not been operated for more than 3 years.** It took 2 hours with a second crew and a hydraulic valve operator.
- 07:10 isolation held; 13:20 repair finished (the pipe was split over almost 2 metres, longer than expected; a section was replaced).
- 15:30 supply restored after flushing.

## Lessons learned
1. **A valve that was not operated for more than 2 years must be treated as stiff.** Plan extra time and name a backup valve before the crew leaves.
2. Include a replacement pipe section and two flexible couplings in the package for old cast iron: the break is usually longer than it looks.
3. A flexible coupling ran out of stock during this incident and arrived late. Keep at least 2 couplings per diameter and reorder after use.
4. Tell residents the time as an estimate and include the flushing time; the first promise was missed by 90 minutes.
5. The care home on the segment was informed late. Inform critical customers first.

## Previous bursts on this segment
This was the third burst on SEG-0412 in 15 years. Renewal of the segment is in the investment plan (simulated).
