# PROC-FLUSH-02: Flushing, disinfection and return to service (INTERNAL)
Versie 1.1 (gesimuleerd) | Owner: Water Quality (simulated)

**This document is internal. Nothing in it is written in public notices. Statements about water quality are never made by a language model.**

## Procedure after a repair
1. Remove the repair section from isolation slowly and open valves in the reverse order of closing.
2. Flush the repaired section until the water is visibly clear.
3. Disinfect the repaired section according to the internal quality plan and take a sample.
4. The return to normal supply is only confirmed by Water Quality, not by the repair crew.

## Planning
- Reserve **60 minutes** for flushing and disinfection after the repair is finished.
- The time told to residents is: repair finished + 60 minutes flushing, rounded up to the next quarter of an hour. This is a safety margin, not a promise of quality.
- Residents are told that the time is an estimate and may change.

## What may be said to residents
- That the supply is expected back around a given time and that this is an estimate.
- Standard advice about temporary cloudiness and the help line is added by the system, not written by the model.
- Anything else about water safety is referred to the help line.

## Samenvatting (NL)
Na de reparatie 60 minuten spoelen en desinfecteren. De tijd voor bewoners is: einde reparatie + 60 minuten, naar boven afgerond op een kwartier, altijd als schatting. Uitspraken over waterkwaliteit staan nooit in publieke berichten.
