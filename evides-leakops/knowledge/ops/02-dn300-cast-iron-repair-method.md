# MS-CI-300: Method statement, burst on a DN300 cast-iron main
Versie 2.0 (gesimuleerd) | Owner: Repair and Maintenance (simulated)

## Scope
Burst (pipe broken open) on cast-iron mains DN300 to DN400, installed before 1980.

## Required crew and equipment
- Technician certified CI-REPAIR-L3, a certified excavator operator and traffic management (arterial roads).
- Excavator of at least 8 tonnes (towed by the service truck), dewatering pump, traffic management kit.

## Method
1. Isolate first (see SOP-ISO-01). Do not start digging before the isolation is confirmed.
2. Set up traffic management before the excavator arrives on an arterial road.
3. Excavate with care. **Old cast iron is brittle and the break is usually longer than it looks.** Expose at least 1 metre on both sides of the visible damage.
4. Choose the repair:
   - **Full-circle repair clamp** when the pipe is cracked or pinholed but not split over a long length.
   - **Replace the section** (ductile iron pipe, 6 m, with two flexible couplings) when the pipe is split lengthwise, or when severity is S1 or S2.
5. Fit, tighten the bolts in a cross pattern and check the gasket seating.
6. Dewater continuously while working. Keep the trench dry.
7. Disinfect and flush before returning to service (see PROC-FLUSH-02).
8. Backfill in layers, compact and restore the surface.

## Expected duration
About 6 hours for the repair itself. Add isolation time, waiting time for parts that are not in the van yet (plus 60 minutes to fit a late part) and the flushing time.

## Common surprises
- A second break close to the first one (the pipe is old and has had earlier bursts).
- A valve that does not hold, so the trench keeps filling. Close the backup valve.
- Frost: ground is hard, allow more excavation time.

## Samenvatting (NL)
Isoleer eerst. Oud gietijzer is bros: leg minimaal 1 meter vrij aan beide kanten. Bij S1 of S2, of een in de lengte gespleten leiding, vervang je de sectie met twee flexibele koppelingen. Droog de sleuf continu en spoel en desinfecteer voor de ingebruikname.
