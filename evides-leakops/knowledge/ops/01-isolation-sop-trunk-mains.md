# SOP-ISO-01: Isolating a burst on a trunk main (DN250 to DN400, cast iron)
Versie 1.3 (gesimuleerd) | Owner: Operations Water Network (simulated)

## Purpose
Close off a burst main safely and quickly so the repair crew can work in a dry trench, while keeping as many customers as possible supplied.

## Procedure
1. **Close both sides of the break.** A break inside a segment must be isolated on both sides. List every isolation valve of the segment and close them in order, the valve nearest the break first.
2. **Count the turns.** A DN300 gate valve needs 120 to 140 turns by hand. Plan 0.1 minute per turn and 5 minutes between valves.
3. **Stiff valves.** A valve that has not been operated for more than 2 years may be stiff or seized. Plan 15 extra minutes and have a **backup valve** identified (the next valve upstream or downstream) before the crew arrives. Use the nearby-valves lookup to find it.
4. **Valves that are not open.** If a valve is not recorded as open, isolation may not hold. Treat it as a risk, name it in the briefing and prepare the backup valve.
5. **Check that the isolation holds.** After closing, confirm that pressure on the isolated side drops. If it does not, close the backup valve.
6. **Critical customers.** If a care home, hospital or dialysis unit is on the isolated section, inform the duty manager first. They arrange alternative supply (water tanker or bottled water) before the valves are closed.
7. **Pressure transients.** Close slowly. Fast closing of large valves can cause pressure surges that break weak old pipes nearby.

## Notes for the diagnostic report
- State which valves, in which order, the number of turns, and the last operation date of each.
- Mention a backup valve whenever a valve is old (over 2 years) or not open.

## Samenvatting (NL)
Sluit beide zijden van de breuk, de dichtstbijzijnde afsluiter eerst. Een afsluiter die langer dan 2 jaar niet is bediend kan vastzitten: reken 15 minuten extra en zoek een reserveafsluiter. Controleer of de isolatie houdt. Informeer eerst de dienstdoende manager als er een zorginstelling op het afgesloten deel zit.
