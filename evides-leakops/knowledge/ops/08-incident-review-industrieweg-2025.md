# INC-2025-0903: Pressure alarm on Industrieweg that was a planned valve replacement (review)
Datum: 3 september 2025 (gesimuleerd) | Segmenten: SEG-0507, SEG-0508

## What happened
At 10:20 a low-pressure alarm fired on PS-201. The control room dispatched a leak crew. On arrival the crew found a contractor replacing valve V-0507-B. The work had been planned and notified to customers, but the planned-works register had not been updated with the start time.

## Findings
- Pressure fell by about 1.1 bar and **the district inlet flow fell by about 45 percent**, the opposite of a leak.
- The planned-works register showed the work as scheduled, not in progress. The crew was sent out for nothing (3 hours of crew time).
- Residents received a notice of a burst that was not one.

## Lessons learned
1. Check the planned works first, and check that the work is **active at the alarm time**, not only scheduled that day.
2. Inflow that falls while pressure falls points to closed valves, not to a leak.
3. The contractor must set the work to "in progress" when starting. Since this incident the register status is part of the alarm check.
4. A false alarm may only be closed with a documented, active planned work. In this incident the status was wrong, so the alarm should have gone to a person (uncertain) instead of being closed by judgement alone.
5. No resident notice is published for a closed false alarm.

## Follow-up
The planned-works register now shows the status in progress, scheduled or completed, and the triage check uses it.
